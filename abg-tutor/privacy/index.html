ABG Tutor

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short

  • ABG Tutor has no accounts and no sign-up.
  • Your lesson progress, points and settings stay on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • The app does not ask about your health and does not collect health data. Every case in it is fictional.
  • If you choose to watch a video to unlock a module, the video is served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data (section 3).
  • Leaderboards (Apple Game Center or Google Play Games) and the purchase are handled by Apple or Google.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we use no analytics tools.

1. Who we are

ABG Tutor (“the app”) is published by Sree Hari Reddy Gadekallu (“we”, “us”). For any question about this policy or your information, email sreehari.kmc@gmail.com.

This policy covers the app on iPhone and iPad (App Store) and on Android (Google Play).

2. Information kept on your device

The app stores these in a database on your device:

  • which lessons you have opened and completed, your answers and number of attempts
  • your points, daily streak and the date you last used the app
  • your settings (text size, sound, haptics) and whether you have read the disclaimer
  • which modules you have unlocked, and whether you bought the full version

We cannot see this information. It is used only to run the app, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads appear only when you choose them: after the free modules, you can watch one video to unlock the next module, or buy the full version instead. Ads are never shown during a lesson, a question or an explanation. Buying the full version removes all ads.

The video is provided by Google AdMob. When an ad loads, Google’s software in the app may collect:

  • device identifiers: on iPhone, the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when asked (otherwise identifiers limited to this app or developer); on Android, the advertising ID
  • your IP address and an approximate location worked out from it
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them
  • app performance and crash information

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and improve its services. Ads are personalized only if you give permission; otherwise Google shows non-personalized ads. The app never sends Google your lesson progress, your answers or anything about your health. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • iPhone and iPad: you can refuse tracking when the app asks, or change it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on the device).
  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for it before ads use your data, and you can change your choice later.
  • You can avoid ads entirely by not watching the videos, or by buying the full version.

4. Leaderboards (Apple Game Center and Google Play Games)

Leaderboards are optional. If you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), the app sends your scores to that service: points this week, all-time points, longest streak and answer efficiency. The service shows them with your player nickname to other players. We do not receive your account details. Apple and Google handle this under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google). To stop sharing scores, sign out of Game Center or Google Play Games in your device settings. Scores already posted are managed through your Apple or Google account.

5. Purchases

The optional full version is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app receives only a confirmation that the purchase is valid and remembers it on your device so it can unlock every module.

6. Emails you send us

“Report an error” in the app opens your own email app with the app’s content version and the last lesson you opened filled in. If you send it, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and to correct the content, and we delete it once your report is dealt with, unless you ask us to keep in touch. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

7. Third parties

The only third parties that receive data through the app are Google (ads; Google Play Games and Google Play billing on Android) and Apple (Game Center and App Store purchases on iPhone and iPad), for the purposes described above. Each processes data under its own privacy policy, which provides protection the same as or equal to this policy. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own. Under some US state laws, personalized advertising can count as “sharing” personal information; you can opt out with the choices in section 3.

8. Keeping and deleting information, and withdrawing consent

  • On your device: deleting the app deletes all its data.
  • Emails: deleted when your report is dealt with, or sooner if you ask.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own retention rules. You can withdraw consent for tracking or personalized ads at any time with the settings in section 3, and manage Google’s data at myaccount.google.com.
  • Leaderboard scores: managed through your Apple or Google account.

To ask us to delete anything we hold about you, email sreehari.kmc@gmail.com. Apart from emails you send, we hold nothing that identifies you.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or US state privacy laws such as California’s), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or limit its use, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email us; we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

The app is made for students and health professionals. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Your progress stays on your device and is protected by your device’s own security. Data that goes to Apple or Google travels over encrypted connections.

12. Education only

ABG Tutor is for education only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Do not enter real patient information into any app or email that is not approved for it.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, mention them in the app’s update notes.
